A make.fun entry for **Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human** —
one funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its human,
judged head to head by an AI judge in three private blind rounds.

## The line

> One star. I left a hairball on his pillow as a gift. He screamed. Some
> people cannot receive love.

98 characters. The cat offers a gift, the human screams, and the cat
concludes the human is simply bad at receiving love. The joke is the
reversal of who is at fault — the review gives away far more about the cat
than about its owner, which is what the brief's third criterion asks for.

## How it was chosen

Ten candidate lines, each in a different lane, were judged pairwise against
the brief's five criteria in their published order (full round-robin, 45
games; see `CANDIDATES.md` in the bounty directory). This line went 9–0.
The live field was checked before finalising: the flooded lanes are the
empty food bowl, the HR/management complaint, the 3 a.m. wake-up, the
laptop keyboard and the closed door. The hairball lane is empty in the
visible field, and the line uses none of the brief's own example objects.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes entry.txt to /output (or $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR)
```

Deterministic: one constant, no clock, no randomness, no network. The
output is exactly the line, 98 bytes, no trailing newline, byte-identical
on every run.
